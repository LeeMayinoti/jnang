Theoretical Background

This graph visualizes the narrative structure of the classic folktale "The Honey and the Flies" through a Resource Description Framework (RDF) ontology based on Vladimir Propp's morphology:

1. Characters & Roles

Individuals in the story (e.g., Glara, Woodpecker, Flies) map directly to Proppian roles (e.g., False Hero, Successful Model, Helper).

2. Narrative Functions

Plot segments and actions (motifemes) that progress the story, starting from the initial lack, the failed imitation, consequences, and rescue.

3. Objects & Magic

Items (e.g., Honey) and supernatural tools/skills (e.g., Whistling Magic) that trigger or resolve narrative functions.

Relationship Types

  • hasRole: Connects a character to their Proppian archetype.
  • performsFunction: Connects a character to a plot point they execute.
  • follows: Represents the chronological flow of narrative events.
  • lacks: Indicates a character's deficiency in objects or skills needed for success.
